# Surface brief: landing page

Scope: `landing/index.html`, the public page at glosa.suchima.com. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: readers of any language who want to learn one by reading their own texts. Action: install and try it (clone, `docker compose up -d`). Proof on hand: the working app and real annotated passages from Lovecraft's "The Tomb"; no users, testimonials or numbers exist and none may be invented. Must not feel: like a SaaS startup (gradients, "start free", sales tone) or childish/gamified (mascots, streaks, toy colours). Copy is bilingual, English by default for an international audience, Spanish when the browser asks for it.

Brand scope this round: new icon, wordmark and identity; the landing is rebuilt in it; the app only takes the icon, favicon and header wordmark (its own UI stays).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one annotated paperback page. glosa explains itself the way its readers read: fluorescent highlighter on the words that matter, pencil glosses in the margin. It refuses the open-source default of a split hero with an app screenshot over a grid of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: a pocket paperback page in cool white with running heads and folios, set in a real book face; three highlighter inks used only to mean something (yellow: the brand and words being learned, blue: new words, pink: expressions); graphite pencil glosses in the margins; neon page flags on the right edge as navigation; a bookmark slip carrying the install commands; the page closes on the book's back cover, drenched in ultramarine.

STORY: the visitor watches a passage being read and annotated, understands that glosa reads their own texts, on their own computer, for free, and copies the install command.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a book page at full width. Top: running head with the wordmark and folio. Left two thirds: the headline set as book text at display size with highlighted words, then a short passage. Right margin: pencil glosses explaining the highlights. A bookmark slip hangs from the top edge on the right with the commands and a copy button, the primary action. Page flags stick out of the right edge.

FORM: Impeccable's pick, rank 1 of my grounded list (annotated paperback); the roll had assigned rank 4 (library card catalogue); seed key 512f75e3.
